# Critical Operational Questions

These are the unanswered questions that most affect whether the 2026 Treble
Makers procedures can be used safely and correctly. Answer them in order. If
you do not know an answer, identify the responsible camp role rather than
guessing.

Do not put personal contact details, payment information, credentials, or
private camper data in this file. Those belong in the runtime materials held
by the Dominatrix.

## Priority 0 — decide before the related activity is allowed

### 1. Which unresolved activities will actually operate in 2026?

For each activity, answer **Operate**, **Do not operate**, or **Undecided**:

- public cheese service;
- flame effects and the Zimmer vaporizer;
- EMT shade and other camp-built structures;
- on-playa generator refueling;
- kitchen propane-cylinder replacement;
- camp shower, fresh-water, and gray-water systems.

An activity marked **Do not operate** can be removed from active checklists.
An activity marked **Undecided** must remain unavailable until its questions
below are resolved.

### 3. What is the approved 2026 flame-effect procedure?

have the Flame Lead supply:

- the physical FAST-license status;
- photographs and exact IDs for every valve and control;
- approved pressure, temperature, voltage, and timing limits;
- startup, test, normal-shutdown, and emergency-shutdown sequences;
- wind limits, exclusion zone, and operator requirements.

Until this exists, the flame task cards are work-package boundaries and do not
authorize operation.

- the authorized operator or provider method;
- the shutdown, cooling, spill-control, and restart method from its manual.

This answer controls `Read_generator_fuel_level` and
`Coordinate_generator_refueling`.
