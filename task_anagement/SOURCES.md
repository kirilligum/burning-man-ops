# Operational research sources

Use current primary guidance for rules and safety limits. Retail products are
provisional, recognizable defaults until the camp records what it actually
owns. Equipment-specific manuals and expert validation override generic advice.

## Kitchen and food service

- [CNHD Burning Man 2026](https://www.centralnevadahd.org/burning-man/) — current
  permit deadline, 2026 food-service guide, and permit exceptions.
- [Burning Man 2026 food-permit guidance](https://survival.burningman.org/rules-and-regulations/health-permits-are-required-for-food-and-beverage-service/)
  — when a camp must obtain a temporary food permit.
- [CNHD temporary-food operational checklist](https://www.centralnevadahd.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/CNHD-Temporary-Food-Application-Final_editsMM01122024.pdf)
  — handwashing, cold holding at 41°F or below, three-stage dishwashing, quat
  sanitizer at 200–400 ppm, test strips, and air drying.

## Leave No Trace, water, and sanitation

- [Burning Man Survival Guide 2026: Leave No Trace tips](https://survival.burningman.org/leave-no-trace/leave-no-trace-tips/)
  — daily line sweeps, anchor recovery, waste sorting, aluminum recycling, and
  porta-potty restrictions.
- [Burning Man 2026 packing and gray-water guidance](https://survival.burningman.org/leave-no-trace/what-to-pack-and-what-to-leave-at-home/)
  — contain all gray water and keep fuel in secondary containment.
- [Burning Man gray-water disposal](https://burningman.org/black-rock-city/preparation/camping-tips/gray-water/)
  — gray-water definition, containment, and disposal options.
- [Burning Man human-waste disposal](https://burningman.org/black-rock-city/preparation/playa-living/human-waste-disposal/)
  — only human waste and one-ply toilet paper belong in porta-potties.
- [Burning Man Survival Guide 2026: on-playa resources](https://survival.burningman.org/city-infrastructure/on-playa-resources/)
  — current Lost and Found handling and Recycle Camp location, hours, and
  accepted empty crushed aluminum cans.
- [Burning Man 2026 camp listings](https://burningman.org/black-rock-city/black-rock-city-2026/2026-camps/)
  — confirms Recycle Camp at 5:35 and Esplanade for 2026.
